# App Store Competitive Positioning

Use when the task asks for ASO framing, category positioning, or competitor-aware metadata.

## Baseline
- Identify the app's core differentiator before drafting metadata.
- Position against the nearest user alternatives, not every app in the category.
- Prefer credible differentiation over exaggerated claims.
- Keep metadata and screenshots consistent with the chosen position.

## Guardrails
- Do not rely on competitor-brand references in storefront copy.
- Do not claim category leadership without evidence.
