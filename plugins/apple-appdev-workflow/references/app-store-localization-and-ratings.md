# App Store Localization and Ratings

Use when ASO work touches regions, localization, ratings, or category expectations.

## Baseline
- Localize meaning, not just wording.
- Keep screenshot text and metadata aligned by locale.
- Treat ratings and reviews as conversion and trust factors when discussing listing quality.
- Note that category norms influence wording, screenshot density, and user expectations.

## Guardrails
- Do not assume English keyword patterns translate directly.
- Do not write copy that becomes fragile under text expansion.
