# App Store Metadata Limits

Use this reference to keep recommendations storefront-compliant.

## Limits
- App Name: 30 characters
- Subtitle: 30 characters
- Promotional Text: 170 characters
- Description: 4000 characters
- Keywords: 100 characters total
- What's New: 4000 characters

## Validation checklist
- Count visible characters, not just words.
- Avoid redundant repetition across short fields.
- Keep keyword lists compact and readable when rendered in guidance.
- Flag any draft that approaches or exceeds a hard storefront limit.
