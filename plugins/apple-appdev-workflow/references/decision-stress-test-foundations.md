# Decision Stress-Test Foundations

Use behind `apple-appdev-workflow:apple-decision-stress-test` for high-value decision review.

## Baseline
- State the claim or decision exactly.
- Steel-man before criticizing.
- Prefer criticizable positions over vague intuitions.
- Focus on what would materially weaken the current conclusion.
- Prefer options that survive criticism while keeping risk and reversibility explicit.

## Default sequence
- What is the decision?
- What problem is it trying to solve?
- What is the strongest case for it?
- What assumptions does it depend on?
- What is the strongest objection?
- What is the best alternative?
- What would change our mind?
- Which parts are reversible vs irreversible?
